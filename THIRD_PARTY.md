## Images and logos in your videos

The skill finds images on Wikimedia Commons and logos from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org)
while it builds an edit. Those are **not** covered by this repository's licence:

- Wikimedia files each carry their own licence, often requiring attribution. The skill records a
  credit for every image it uses; put those credits in your video description.
- Brand logos are trademarks of their owners. Simple Icons' [legal disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md)
  applies; check a brand's guidelines before using its mark.
